FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A little more clarity.

Good questions deserve honest answers.

Where is Bestower based?

Bestower Enterprises is based in Perambur, Chennai, Tamil Nadu. Our focus is premium Indian specialty foods with an India-wide ecommerce experience.

Where does the saffron come from?

Our Kashmiri Mongra saffron uses Pampore, Kashmir as its origin reference. The initial product is a 1 g selection with an MRP reference of ₹599. Contact us for final batch and label information.

Are the product photographs actual pack photographs?

Not yet. This review edition uses illustrative product and landscape imagery. Official Bestower packaging and product photographs will be added before launch.

What pack sizes are available?

Saffron is presented as 1 g. Other product pack sizes, ingredient labels and prices need final approval. Where a product has confirmed variants, choose the pack on its product page.

Do you deliver across India?

India-wide ecommerce is our focus. PIN-code serviceability, shipping charges and dispatch estimates must be confirmed with Bestower before launch and before an order is placed.

Which payment methods can I use?

Available payment methods are displayed by the connected store at checkout. Online payments require the business to complete its payment-provider setup before launch. This review edition does not claim that UPI, cards or cash on delivery are already enabled.

Can I ask about a product before buying?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp +91 9962630550, or email business@bestower.co.in. Please include the product name and your question.

Are there any health or medicinal claims?

No. Bestower’s presentation is based on food, origin and thoughtful selection. No medicinal, therapeutic or exaggerated health claims are made. Check final labels for ingredients and allergens.
